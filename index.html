04 INTERNSHIP WORKFLOW

From a question to clear direction.

A transparent, repeatable workflow that keeps the analysis connected to the decision it supports.

  1. 01

    Understand the business problem

    Clarify the stakeholder, decision, scope, and success measures.

  2. 02

    Collect & inspect the dataset

    Check grain, fields, coverage, assumptions, and data quality.

  3. 03

    Clean & preprocess data

    Resolve duplicates, missing values, inconsistent formats, and outliers.

  4. 04

    Explore patterns & calculate KPIs

    Use EDA to find relationships, trends, and questions worth testing.

  5. 05

    Build visuals, insights & final report

    Present a careful narrative with recommendations and limitations.
